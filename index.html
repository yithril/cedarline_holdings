About

You provide the space. We run the machine.

Cedar Line Holdings is a Michigan-based vending operator serving businesses in Southeast Michigan. We place the machine, pick the products, keep it stocked and clean, and handle repairs and payment problems when they come up.

There is nothing for your staff to manage. No inventory to order, no cash to count, no service calls to schedule. For locations that are a good fit, placement comes at no cost to the business.

How it works

Getting started takes three steps.

No long procurement process and no equipment for you to buy.

  1. STEP 01

    You reach out

    Tell us the type of business, roughly how many people are there each day, and where you are located.

  2. STEP 02

    We look at the location

    We confirm it is a fit, then recommend snacks, beverages, or both based on your space and your traffic.

  3. STEP 03

    We install and service it

    We deliver and set up the machine, then keep it stocked, clean, and working on an ongoing schedule.

Services

Vending machine service, start to finish.

Vending machine placement and ongoing service for offices, warehouses, auto shops, gyms, retail spaces, and multi-tenant properties.

Machine placement

We look at your space, your traffic, and who is there day to day, then install equipment that fits. Snacks, beverages, or both. For locations that qualify, there is no equipment cost to you.

Stocking and product selection

We keep the machine filled with what people at your location actually buy. Popular snacks and beverages, energy drinks, and lighter options where there is demand. We watch what sells, adjust the mix, and take requests.

Service and repairs

If a machine jams, stops taking payment, or needs cleaning, that is ours to fix. One call or email and we take care of it. Machines are set up to accept cards and mobile payment alongside cash.
